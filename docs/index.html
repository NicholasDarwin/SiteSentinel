Every verdict shows its evidence.

        
A score on its own asks you to take it on faith. Open any check in a report and you get what SiteSentinel actually saw: the response headers it received, the line of HTML that triggered a warning, the script and where it loads from.

        
Each finding also says how many points it cost, why it matters and how to fix it, with a note when the result could be a false positive.

How a score is counted.

        
Every category starts at 100. A failed check costs its full weight, a warning costs half. Information-only checks cost nothing.

      


      

        

          
Critical
35 points

        

        

          
High
20 points

        

        

          
Medium
10 points

        

        

          
Low
5 points

        

      


      
A category never drops below 15 from ordinary findings, so one missing header can't read as total failure. The exception is a confirmed malware or phishing match, which sets Safety & Threats to 0.
